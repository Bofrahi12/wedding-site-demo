Our story

How We Met

◆

It began on a rainy Tuesday in San Francisco — a shared umbrella, a missed bus, and a conversation neither of us wanted to end. What started as a chance encounter over coffee turned into morning hikes, late-night cooking experiments, and a thousand small moments that felt like home.

Five years later, on a cliff above the Pacific at sunset, James got down on one knee. Through happy tears, Sophia said yes — and now we can't wait to celebrate with the people we love most.
