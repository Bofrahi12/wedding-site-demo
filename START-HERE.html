🤍 Your Wedding Website — Guide

Editing & publishing, step by step. No coding experience needed.

1. What's in your ZIP

📁index.html — all the text on your site (names, dates, story…)
🎨styles.css — colors and fonts
⚙️script.js — countdown date + your RSVP email
🖼️images/ — the photos (replace with yours)

2. Preview it first

1Unzip the folder.
2Double-click index.html — it opens in your browser. That's your site!

3. Make it yours

ANames, dates, story — open index.html in any text editor (Notepad, TextEdit). Search for Sophia and replace with your names, June 12, 2027 with your date, and rewrite the story paragraphs. Save.
BPhotos — put your photos in the images/ folder and rename them couple.webp and venue.webp (or any .jpg — then update the two file names inside index.html).
CColors — open styles.css, find the :root block at the top and change the color codes, e.g. --sage:#7C8B6F.
DCountdown date — open script.js, find 2027-06-12T16:30:00 and set your wedding date & time.

4. Get RSVPs in your email ★ important

1In script.js, find const RSVP_EMAIL = "youremail@example.com" and put your email address between the quotes. Save.
2Open index.html in your browser, fill in the RSVP form once with a test reply and submit.
3Check your inbox — you'll get an activation email from FormSubmit. Click the activation link.
4Done! Every guest RSVP now arrives straight in your inbox. (Free — no account needed.)

5. Publish it free (5 minutes)

1Go to app.netlify.com/drop.
2Drag your unzipped website folder onto the page.
3Netlify gives you a free link like your-names.netlify.app — rename it if you like.
4Share that link with your guests! (Want your own domain? Netlify → Domain settings.)
📱 Tip: open your published link on your phone — 80% of guests will view it there. Everything is mobile-friendly.

6. FAQ

Can I change the fonts? Yes — the fonts load from Google Fonts at the top of index.html; swap the names.

Can I remove a section? Yes — each section in index.html is clearly labeled (<!-- STORY --> etc.). Delete the block you don't need.

Something broke? Re-download your original ZIP from Etsy and start that step over — you can't break anything permanently.
